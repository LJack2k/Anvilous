First release.

Anvilous moves repairing, combining and enchanting off the anvil and onto the vanilla
smithing table, where they cost materials instead of experience, and where nothing gets
more expensive the more you use it. No new blocks and no new menus.

**Repairing** takes a worn item and a material and restores a set percentage of maximum
durability per material item, spending only as many as the repair needs.

**Combining** adds two worn copies of an item together, plus vanilla's 12% bonus, and
merges their enchantments.

**Enchanting** applies a book for a price in materials, charged per level.

Enchantments and components survive all of it, nothing costs experience, and the hidden
anvil counter behind *Too Expensive!* is reset rather than raised.

### Out of the box

169 recipes covering every damageable item and every enchantment in vanilla. The numbers
are not a new balance: each repair uses the material vanilla's own anvil already accepts
at the same 25% it already restores, every damageable item can be combined because the
anvil combines any two of the same item, and each enchantment is priced from its
`anvil_cost`.

### For pack makers

The recipes ship as three datapacks - repair, combine and enchanting - each switchable
off in `config/anvilous-common.toml`, so a pack can keep some, override any single one by
path, or start from nothing and ship only its own.

The generators that produced them ship too, behind `[tools] commands`. They read the
loaded registries, so inside a modpack they write correct recipes for its own tools and
enchantments. See `docs/pack-makers.md`.

### Viewers

Recipes show up in JEI, EMI and REI, each through a plugin of its own. None is required.

### Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge, needed on both client and server.
